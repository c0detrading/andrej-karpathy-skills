# TattooBar: what the user corrected, round by round

TattooBar is a tattoo studio and cocktail bar at Royal Wharf, London E16, with artist Vilma. The site is in `volstro/tattoobar/`. Each round lists what I sent, what the user changed, and the rule it became (see `house-style.md`). Use it to spot the same mistakes early.

| Round | What I sent | What the user said | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Approval pack | Two directions: A "Moon Room" (dark, Bodoni serif) and B "Cast" (light concrete, Archivo Expanded, centre seam) | Liked B, not the fonts. Sent four references: Saints, Ethan Carter, Vibe Ink's Instagram, iPad hand lettering | Ask for references first. Fonts are a separate choice. |
| 2. Fonts | Three pairings on the Cast hero | "Change the layout, choose something similar to what I uploaded." Big Shoulders for logo and big words, "only use two fonts" | Layouts come from the user's references. Two fonts. |

Layout | Saints-style logo, repeated words behind a photo, Vibe Ink tiles, Ethan Carter section titles | Logo more visible, less opacity behind the photo, outside margins 20–30 px, the same 60–100 px between every section | Solid logo, background text at about 20%, fixed margins and spacing tokens. |
| 4. First build | Six pages with a long homepage | "Too much on the home page." Sent a wireframe of the home page and a tattoo and piercing page. Prices button in the header, no Visit button | Short homepage, a page per offer, build to their sketch. |
| 5. Markup PDF (31 pages) | Paged A4 PDF | "Biggest issue is the 4 grid system, you kinda forgot about it." Bigger buttons, more Vilma text, stars on reviews, phone number fix, style filter back, taller happy hour panels | One grid on every page, big buttons, stars. |

## What would have saved the most time

1. **Asking for references and a homepage sketch in the first reply.** Rounds 1–4 came down to the user showing me what they wanted.
2. **Building on one grid from the start**, with every component spanning whole columns and sharing one inset and one gutter. Rounds 5, 7, 8 and 10 were mostly alignment.
3. **Checking alignment myself with grid screenshots before sending**, so the user doesn't have to draw the lines.
4. **Sending the single long markup PDF every round** from round 1.
5. **Listing the facts to confirm** (handles, phone numbers, schedules) up front instead of trusting scraped pages.
