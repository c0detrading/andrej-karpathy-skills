Book the course

3 days with Vilma, £850.

Do I need any experience?

No. This is a beginner course for complete newcomers with no tattooing experience.

Will I tattoo real skin?

No. All practice is on synthetic practice skin, for training and safety.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You receive a TattooBar Certificate of Completion when you finish the course.

Do I need to bring anything?

All the essential training equipment is provided. You may be advised on optional personal items after booking.

Are deposits refundable?

No. Deposits are non-refundable, as they secure your place and cover course resources.

Is there an age limit?

Yes. You must be 18 or over.

Community programme

Creative Connections is a free 12-week programme for adults that brings people together through creativity, art and beginner tattoo design.

Each week mixes creative wellbeing workshops with beginner tattoo design sessions. All practice is on paper and synthetic skin. Nobody gets tattooed.
