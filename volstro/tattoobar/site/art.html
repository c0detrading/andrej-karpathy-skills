The art and the place

When creating this interior, we aimed to break away from the ordinary and offer something unlike any other place. Every detail was chosen to make a visit memorable.

Vilma

Vilma began her artistic journey at art school, painting angels, demons and fantasy worlds.

That theme has always stayed close to her heart and fills her work with magic and mysticism. Her real passion showed itself when she met a tattoo artist who recognised the talent in her hands. He suggested she try tattooing, and from that moment her life changed.

Tattooing became her calling. She wants every client to leave with a unique piece of art made with love and skill. To her, a tattoo is more than an image on the skin: it is a form of self-expression that should bring joy.

Vilma on Instagram

The idea

The idea to combine a bar and a tattoo studio came from conversations with clients.

Many said that after getting a tattoo they wanted a cocktail, a well-deserved reward for the pain. TattooBar brings both together in one cosy room: a place where pain and pleasure meet.

The Guardian

The Guardian of the Bar

Vilma

Sculpture

As soon as Vilma saw the empty wall, she knew a Guardian belonged there. A figure of strength, calm and timeless femininity, she gazes into eternity and shields the bar from the hustle and bustle of the outside world.

The counter

TattooBar counter sculpture

Vilma

24 tattoo machines from 20 years of tattooing, set into the bar counter

The TattooBar inscription marks her years of dedication to tattooing. Every detail tells a story, and the counter holds her memories, her experience and her love for art.

The Moon

The Moon

Vilma

The idea came when Vilma realised the room, in its cool tones, felt like a dive into space. The glowing Moon invites you to reach for the stars just by walking in, so every visit is a small journey to the Moon.
