Before and after

How to get your skin ready for your session, and how to look after your new tattoo.

Before

  • Get hydrated and moisturise
  • Shave and exfoliate
  • Eat before the session
  • Wear comfortable, suitable clothes
  • Avoid drugs and certain products before the session

Aftercare

  1. Remove the bandage after 1 to 3 hours.
  2. Wash with soap and water, using antibacterial soap.
  3. Apply a small amount of antibacterial ointment and pat dry with a towel.
  4. Do this at least 3 times a day for 3 days.
  5. After 3 days, use a non-perfumed hand lotion.

While it heals

  • Don't swim or soak it in water
  • Don't touch it except to wash it
  • Don't let anyone else touch it
  • Don't pick at any scabs
  • Keep it out of the sun for 2 weeks

Don't use

  • Neosporin
  • Peroxide
  • Alcohol
  • Olive oil
  • Corn oil
