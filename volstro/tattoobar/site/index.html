Vilma

Hey, I'm Vilma, and my main inspiration is you.

Vilma started at art school, painting angels, demons and fantasy worlds. A tattoo artist saw the talent in her hands and suggested she try tattooing. She has been tattooing for over 20 years since, creating unique, personal designs for every client.

She keeps to the highest standards of hygiene, and keeps learning through courses, workshops and conventions.

The idea for TattooBar came from her clients. After a tattoo, they wanted a cocktail as a reward for the pain. So she put a bar in the studio.
