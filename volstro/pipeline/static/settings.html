Instagram and Facebook (Meta)

The app reads followers, posts, likes and comments from Meta once a day with an access token. Instagram works for any business or creator account, so prospects too, with no permission needed from them. Facebook works for Pages Volstro has access to. Set it up once (about 15 minutes):

  1. Make sure Volstro's Instagram is a business or creator account, linked to Volstro's Facebook Page.
  2. At developers.facebook.com → My Apps → Create App, create an app of type Business and connect it to Volstro's business portfolio.
  3. At business.facebook.com/settings → Users → System users, add an Admin system user. Under Assign assets give it the app, Volstro's Page, and every client Page you want Facebook numbers for (a client adds Volstro as a partner on their Page first).
  4. Click Generate new token, pick the app, set expiry to Never and tick instagram_basic, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management.
  5. Paste the token below, click Save settings, then Check connection.

Daily report

If the app isn't running at that time, the report is made as soon as it starts.

Import numbers from a CSV

For TikTok (or any account) you can type the numbers into a spreadsheet and import them all at once. Rows are matched to clients by platform and handle. Only platform, handle and followers are required. A missing date means today.
