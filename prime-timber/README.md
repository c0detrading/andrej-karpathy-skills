# Prime Timber USA: redesign concept

A one-page redesign concept of [primetimberusa.com](https://primetimberusa.com/), made by VOLSTRO as a pitch. It isn't the official site, and a bar at the top of the page says so.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It's a single file with no build step.

## What it uses

Everything factual comes from their current site: the four products and their specs, the stock figure, production figures, ordering steps, contact details and the customer projects. The colours (warm black, moss, sage) and the stacked-layers logo come from their existing brand.

The wood panels are drawn in code, so no photos were copied. For a real build, swap in their product and project photography.

## What changes from the current site

- **One page instead of five.** The four application pages mostly repeat the homepage, so each application is now a tab with its own customer projects.
- **Specs up front.** Stock, thicknesses, lengths and minimum order sit directly under the hero. Each product card lists its specs and has a quote button that preselects that product in the form.
- **Full stave vs finger-jointed explained**, with a panel drawing in the hero that switches between the four products.
- **A more useful quote form**: product, quantity, thickness, delivery or pickup, and project details.

## Problems on the current site worth raising

- Russian text is left in the page: "Продукция" in the menu, and "Сообщение об успешной отправке!" as the form success message.
- Maximum length is contradictory. The product cards say 13′ 9″, while the countertops, millwork and DIY pages say 13′ 1″. The homepage applications section appears twice (apparently desktop and mobile versions), once with each figure. This concept uses 13′ 9″ from the product cards. **Confirm with the client.**
- Product cards show a bare "$" with no price.
- The phone number is formatted two ways (+1 786 879 4425 and +1 786 879 44 25).
- One millwork project is labelled "Ash Full Stave Panel" but described as oak wall paneling.
